## App name

Joon: fewer, better emails

## Subtitle

State-aware email campaigns and journeys, prepared for your approval.

## Description

Joon connects to Shopify, learns your brand and builds a living picture of each customer from permitted order and engagement data. It prepares email campaigns and journeys, explains who should receive each campaign and deliberately leaves customers alone when the message is unlikely to help.

Campaign controls are random and separate from Joon's state-based restraint. They provide pooled evidence over time; they do not determine the invoice. Journeys have no random control assignment and reach every eligible entrant under their consent, safety and exit rules.

Early access is free. No Shopify billing charge is created. Any billing preview is illustrative and clearly labelled.

## Features

- Customer state from order rhythm, lifecycle, category and real discount evidence
- On-brand email drafts with merchant approval before campaigns send
- Welcome, abandoned-checkout, browse, post-purchase, reorder and win-back journeys
- “Left alone by Joon” decisions with evidence and reconsideration timing
- Random campaign controls and evidence-strength labels
- Attributed revenue and delivery outcomes
