## App name

Joon

## Subtitle

Approval-first email that measures what it caused

## Short description

Joon prepares fewer, better emails from your Shopify data, keeps every send under your approval and measures the result against customers held back at random.

## Listing description

Joon works through your store and customer history before it asks you to send anything.

It prepares the audience, email, offer and timing. You see who will receive it, who is left alone and why, and the exact version waiting for approval. Nothing sends until you approve it.

Afterward, Joon compares emailed customers with a random held-back control. That separates revenue the email likely caused from purchases that would have happened anyway.

Use Joon to:

- Prepare email campaigns in your brand voice.
- Run six merchant-activated email journeys.
- Apply consent, suppression, fatigue and recent-purchase safeguards.
- Hold back a random control from the eligible audience.
- Freeze the audience and content you approve.
- See attributed revenue and measured caused revenue separately.
- Record a reason for every send, exclusion, deferral and control assignment.

Public v1 sends email only. SMS consent can be captured separately for future use, but Joon does not send SMS, WhatsApp or RCS today.

## Pricing

**Free during early access.**

No subscription or usage charge is currently collected. Joon may show a shadow billing preview so you can understand the proposed future model, but it makes no billing API calls.

The planned model charges a performance fee only on measurement-ready caused revenue, plus provider postage at cost for blasts you ask Joon to send. It is not active and may change before billing launches.

## Primary benefit

Know what your email changed - not only what happened after it.
