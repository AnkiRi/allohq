## Post 1 - the bill

Most email tools get paid when you add contacts and send more.

Joon is built around the opposite question: which email is worth sending at all?

It holds back a few eligible customers at random. If the emailed group does not spend more than the held-back group, there is no measured lift.

During early access, Joon is free. The calculator shows the proposed model after that: Joon keeps ₹1 of every ₹5 it can show it caused. You keep ₹4.

See an illustrative scenario: `[calculator URL]?subs=70000&rev=1500000#bill`

## Post 2 - attributed is not caused

A customer bought after an email.

That does not mean the email caused the purchase.

Joon shows both numbers: what happened after email, and what changed versus a random control.

The second number is smaller. It is also the honest one.

See what that changes in the bill: `[calculator URL]?subs=30000&rev=800000#bill`

## Post 3 - left alone

The interesting part of an email campaign is not only who received it.

It is who did not:

Recently purchased. Fatigue cap. Quiet hours - send at 07:00 local. Random control.

Different reasons. All visible before approval.

Joon is the email tool that gets paid to send less.

Try an illustrative store: `[calculator URL]?subs=100000&rev=3000000#bill`

## Post 4 - no lift

What if Joon sends a campaign and it creates no lift?

Then there is no performance fee.

That sounds obvious. It is not how email software is usually priced.

Joon compares emailed customers with customers held back at random, and future performance pricing uses only measurement-ready caused revenue. Early access remains free.

Change the caused-share assumption to zero: `[calculator URL]?subs=70000&rev=1500000&caused=0#bill`

## Post 5 - the cap

Outcome pricing should not turn one successful month into an alarming invoice.

Joon's planned performance fee is capped at the approved published plan price for a comparable leading email platform. Blasts you request carry provider postage at cost. Joon does not profit from sending.

No wallet. No recharge balance. One clear monthly view.

Compare an illustrative high-volume store: `[calculator URL]?subs=250000&rev=10000000#bill`
