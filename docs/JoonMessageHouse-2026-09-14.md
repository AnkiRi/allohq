# Joon message house

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Status:** Current decision record for public, Shopify-listing and founder copy  
**Supersedes:** `JoonMessageHouse-2026-09-11.md` wherever pricing, controls or restraint conflict

## Position

The email tool that gets paid to send less.

Joon saves a changing customer state, decides when a campaign may help, prepares the exact email for approval and makes deliberate restraint visible.

## Pricing

Early access is free. After early access, the displayed proposal is 5% of non-cancelled order revenue attributed to an email Joon actually sent within seven days. Shadow invoices also compute 6% and 8% for founder testing. If attributed revenue is zero, the fee is zero. Sending and the email creator are included. The total is capped at the sourced comparable Klaviyo email-plan tier for the active list.

Never describe the invoice as a lift fee, caused-revenue share, postage, subscription, per-contact or per-send charge. Random-control evidence does not enter invoice arithmetic.

## Audience language

- **Subscribed audience:** legally contactable by email.
- **Campaign candidate:** Joon's current state-based policy says this campaign may help.
- **Deliberately left alone:** Joon decided this customer does not need this campaign. The decision changes when the customer's state changes.
- **Control group:** a random, rotating sample of campaign candidates not sent this campaign.
- **Treatment group:** remaining candidates assigned the campaign.
- **Deferred:** should receive it later because of quiet hours or scheduling.
- **Sent:** accepted by the provider.

Never use “suppressed” as the primary merchant label for deliberate restraint; reserve suppression for legal, consent and delivery blocks in operational contexts. Never call deliberate restraint a holdout. Never describe a control assignment as intelligence.

## Proof

Controls apply to measurable campaigns only, after deliberate restraint. Journeys have no random control and reach every eligible entrant under consent, safety, fatigue, quiet-hour and purchase-exit rules. Campaign evidence is directional until pooled evidence supports a stronger claim. Lift is proof and a learning input, never the invoice.

## Human evidence language

Prefer: “All 10 of Maya's previous orders were at full price. She may not need this code.”  
Avoid: “Maya will buy without a discount.”

For a discount request, explain the alternative: “Maya, ABC, DEF and seven others usually buy at full price. Include them here anyway, or create a separate full-price message for them.”

## Required honesty

Illustrative models are labelled. No fabricated customers, outcomes or testimonials. Comparisons use named, dated, linked primary price evidence. Public v1 is email-only. Joon does not claim that attributed revenue is caused revenue.
