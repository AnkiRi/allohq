## Joon: AI Email & Holdouts

Build on-brand email campaigns and journeys, approve every send, and measure lift against a real holdout.

Joon connects to Shopify, learns your brand settings, prepares campaigns and merchant-activated journeys, resolves a consent-safe audience and shows exactly who will receive the email. Eligible campaigns reserve a randomized control by default. Small cohorts are clearly labeled unmeasured. Nothing sends without approval.

Features: natural-language campaign plans; Shopify segments and discounts; brand-aware email; six journey families; consent/suppression/fatigue exclusions; frozen control assignment; treatment/control reporting; Shopify data and storefront-event sync. Email-only v1.

Screenshot sequence: prompt and plan; email and brand kit; audience exclusions; 50 eligible → 43 treatment/7 control; journey builder; decision ledger. Use a clearly marked demo store only—no invented merchant logos or performance claims.
